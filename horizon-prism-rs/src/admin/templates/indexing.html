Public data

What Prism indexes. 'Kryphocron only' keeps only what kryphocron posts and Horizon features need (blocks, follows involving members, profiles, members' own records), and Prism stops answering public Bluesky requests. Switching to it deletes the stored public data in the background. 'Community' keeps public posts and interactions of your members and the accounts around them; switching to it refills them, and public requests come back when the refill is done. 'Full network' stores the public network as well, for the record groups set below, and crawls each account's history; the page that confirms it says how fast the database grows and how long the disk lasts. Leaving it deletes what it added.
